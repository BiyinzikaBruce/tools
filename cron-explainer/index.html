Cron Expression Explainer

Type a cron schedule to see what it means in plain English and the next times it will run in your local time. Handy for Vercel cron jobs and server tasks.

FreeRuns in your browser · nothing is uploaded

  

    

┌──────── minute        0-59
│ ┌────── hour          0-23
│ │ ┌──── day of month  1-31
│ │ │ ┌── month         1-12 or JAN-DEC
│ │ │ │ ┌ day of week   0-6 (Sun=0) or SUN-SAT
* * * * *

*      any value         5,10   list
1-5    range             */15   every 15
@daily @hourly @weekly @monthly @yearly

  

  
Vercel and most servers run cron in UTC. If you are in East Africa (UTC+3), "0 6 * * *" in UTC runs at 9:00 your time.
